# Fredrik Armerin

Welcome to my academic website.

I am an academic researcher with interests in economics,
probability theory, stochastic analysis, and mathematical
finance.

My research focuses on mathematical methods for understanding
decision-making under uncertainty, optimal investment, and
the role of information and ambiguity in economic models.

## Research interests

My research interests include:

- **Decision-making under uncertainty:** Risk, ambiguity,
  and robust decision-making.
- **Optimal investment:** Real options, irreversible investment,
  and investment timing under uncertainty.
- **Probability theory:** Martingales, stochastic processes,
  and stochastic calculus.
- **Information theory:** Relative entropy, divergence measures,
  and their applications in probability and economics.
- **Mathematical finance:** Stochastic modelling and the
  valuation of financial and real options.

## Research

My research explores mathematical approaches to uncertainty
in economic decision-making, with particular interest in
ambiguity and optimal investment.

One of my research topics is the optimal timing of irreversible
investment when the decision-maker faces ambiguity about the
underlying economic environment.

The analysis connects real-options theory with robust
decision-making and information-theoretic measures of
uncertainty.
